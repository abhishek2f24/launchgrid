Privacy Policy

App: Dream Life • Application ID: in.launchgrid.dreamlife • Last Updated: October 8, 2026

Dream Life shows rough estimates to motivate you. It is not financial advice; real prices, taxes and returns vary.

1. What the app does

Dream Life, published by LaunchGrid, adds up what your dream life would cost and estimates when you could afford it.

2. Your data stays on your phone

  • Your name, dream boards, chosen currency, and the savings and investment numbers you enter are stored only on your phone. They are never uploaded.
  • Share cards are shared only when you tap Share, through Android's share sheet.

3. Crash reports and notifications

The app uses two Google Firebase services, operated by Google on our behalf:

  • Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report when the app crashes: the stack trace, device model, Android version, app version and a random installation identifier. It contains none of your content. You can turn crash reports off in the app's Settings.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging gives your device a random token so we can send occasional notifications about the app. You can turn these off in the app's Settings or in Android's notification settings.

Reminders you switch on are scheduled on your phone and need no server. Data sent to Firebase is encrypted in transit. Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days.

4. Purchases

Pro subscriptions and the lifetime unlock are sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment; we never see your card or payment details. The app asks Google Play which purchases your account owns and checks them on your device. We do not keep our own record of your purchases.

5. What we don't do

  • No account or sign-in, and no advertising or ad identifiers.
  • We don't sell, rent or share your data, and we don't use it to profile you.
  • No analytics beyond the crash reports above.

6. Children

The app is meant for people aged 18 and over and is not directed at children under 13.

7. Deleting your data

Everything you create lives in the app's private storage on your phone, so you can delete it yourself at any time:

  1. Open Android Settings → Apps → Dream Life → Storage and tap Clear storage, or simply uninstall the app. This permanently deletes everything the app stored.
  2. To also have the crash reports and notification token linked to your installation deleted from Firebase, email support@launchgrid.in with the subject "Delete my Dream Life data". We delete them within 30 days.

Crash reports are deleted automatically after 90 days even if you don't ask. Files you chose to save to your gallery or share stay where you put them.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy: support@launchgrid.in
