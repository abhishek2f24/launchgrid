--- TABLE 2 ---
Scenario | Required Checks | Extra Recommended Checks
Used phone/laptop | UPI/bank name consistency, seller phone, invoice name, IMEI/serial, product photo metadata where available | Video call with product live, battery health, original bill, face-to-face inspection
Vehicle buying | Seller identity consistency, vehicle number, RC details where legally available, payment recipient match | Service history, challans, insurance, hypothecation, physical inspection
Rental deposit | Owner/agent identity consistency, property address, rent agreement draft, payment recipient match | Meet owner, verify property possession, avoid token without visit
Freelance/client | Company domain, GSTIN if business, email domain, contract, payment terms | Advance payment, milestone escrow, LinkedIn/company verification
Business supplier | GSTIN, PAN/name consistency, bank name, website/domain age signal, invoice format | Physical address, sample order, trade references
Job/investment offer | Fee demand, unrealistic return/salary, fake HR email, domain mismatch | Official career page, no payment for job, SEBI/RBI regulated entity check
--- TABLE 3 ---
Signal | Example | Score Impact
Identity/payment mismatch | Seller says name A, QR/account shows name B | High negative
Pressure tactic | Pay in 10 minutes or deal cancelled | Medium-high negative
Refuses verification | No video call, no invoice, no inspection | Medium negative
High amount, low evidence | Rs. 50,000+ with only WhatsApp chat | High negative
Consistent proof | Name consistent across invoice, UPI, phone and chat | Positive
Known scam pattern | Courier pickup overpayment, fake army/CISF seller, job registration fee | High negative

--- TABLE 5 ---
Layer | Recommendation
Frontend | React Native or Flutter for Android/iOS; Next.js for web and admin
Backend | Node.js/NestJS or Python/FastAPI; PostgreSQL; Redis queue
AI/OCR | Document OCR, QR parsing, LLM-based chat red-flag extraction with deterministic rule engine for scoring
Storage | India-region cloud storage if available; encrypted object storage for evidence
Payments | Razorpay/PayU/Cashfree for Rs. 99 report payments; do not hold counterparty funds in MVP
Analytics | PostHog/Mixpanel, server-side event tracking, fraud taxonomy dashboard
Security | Vault/KMS, WAF, audit logging, vulnerability scanning, CERT-In compliant logs
